## Haven Bounceback


One day, the surface of H-103 suddenly went dark.
Nobody knew why. Nobody knew what had happened. And when the light finally returned, humanity was greeted by a sight it could never have imagined.
The planet was dying.
The once-vibrant landscapes had become barren wastelands. Forests disappeared, rivers dried up, and life slowly faded from the surface.
Humanity fought back. They tried everything to preserve what remained, but their efforts could only save a tiny fraction of the planet.
That last patch of greenery became humanity's final refuge.
They called it HAVEN-REFORM.
A year after the last group of survivors settled there, something extraordinary happened.
A portal appeared.
Nobody knew where it led. Nobody knew what was on the other side. But it offered something humanity hadn't seen in years.
Hope.
The survivors were amazed, but they were also exhausted. They had spent years fighting a losing battle, and most no longer believed the planet could ever recover.
But you weren't like them.
You had always believed that no matter how many times you were knocked down, you could always get back up.
And perhaps this was no different.
So you stepped through the portal.
This time, however, the stakes were different.
You weren't fighting for yourself.
You were fighting for the future of humanity.
